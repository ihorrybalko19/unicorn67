# Erasmus+ Office FAQ (2026/2027)

<!-- Demo data: Nordbaltic University, its staff and these rules are fictional. -->

1. **When is the deadline for the insurance document?**
   Please send your health and liability insurance document before **15 January** for the Spring semester (or **15 August** for the Autumn semester).

2. **Who signs my Learning Agreement (LA)?**
   Your Learning Agreement must be signed by your departmental Erasmus coordinator at your home university first, and then by the receiving coordinator at the host university. At Nordbaltic University the departmental Erasmus coordinator is **Dr. Laura Ozoliņa** (l.ozolina@nordbaltic.example).

3. **How do I confirm my arrival?**
   Within 5 days of arriving at your host university, have the host international office sign your **Certificate of Arrival** and email the scan to us.

4. **What are the required documents for every Erasmus student?**
   Every student must submit 4 documents: `LearningAgreement`, `Transcript`, `Insurance`, and `CertificateOfArrival`.

5. **How many ECTS must my Transcript of Records show?**
   Before the mobility, your transcript must show at least **30 ECTS** completed in the previous academic year. After the mobility, the host university's transcript must show at least **20 ECTS per semester**; with fewer credits, part of the grant may have to be repaid.

6. **When is the Erasmus grant paid?**
   The first instalment (**70%**) is paid by **15 October** for the Autumn semester and by **15 March** for the Spring semester, provided your Learning Agreement and Insurance have been received. The remaining **30%** is paid within **45 days** after you submit your final Transcript of Records.
