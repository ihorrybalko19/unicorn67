# Erasmus+ Office FAQ (2026/2027)

1. **When is the deadline for the insurance document?**
   Please send your health and liability insurance document before **15 January** for the Spring semester (or **15 August** for the Autumn semester).

2. **Who signs my Learning Agreement (LA)?**
   Your Learning Agreement must be signed by your departmental Erasmus coordinator at your home university first, and then by the receiving coordinator at the host university.

3. **How do I confirm my arrival?**
   Within 5 days of arriving at your host university, have the host international office sign your **Certificate of Arrival** and email the scan to us.

4. **What are the required documents for every Erasmus student?**
   Every student must submit 4 documents: `LearningAgreement`, `Transcript`, `Insurance`, and `CertificateOfArrival`.
